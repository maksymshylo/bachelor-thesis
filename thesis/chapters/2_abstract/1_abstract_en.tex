\chapter*{Abstract}

The aim is to develop a model for obtaining cloud-free composites
of satellite images. Sentinel-2 data from the Copernicus Open Access Hub and the
Kyiv region are studied. Cloud masks, image-combination methods, and regression
models for colour transfer between incomplete neighbouring regions are
investigated. The best quality among the tested models was obtained with a multilayer
perceptron.

Keywords:
